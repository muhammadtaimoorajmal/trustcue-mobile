TrustCue Account Deletion

Permanent Account and Cloud Data Removal

Application: TrustCue (io.github.muhammadtaimoorajmal.trustcue)

Policy Compliance: Google Play User Data Policy – Account Deletion Requirement

TrustCue provides two direct, discoverable pathways for users to delete their account and associated data.

Pathway 1: In-App Deletion (Instant & Automated)

If you have the TrustCue app installed on your Android device:

  1. Open the TrustCue app.
  2. Tap the Account icon in the top navigation bar.
  3. Sign in if prompted.
  4. Scroll to the bottom of the Account screen and tap Delete Account.
  5. Review the permanent deletion disclosures.
  6. Re-enter your account password to verify ownership.
  7. Tap Permanently Delete Account.

Result: Your account is immediately erased on the server via an atomic database cascade (ON DELETE CASCADE). All auth records, user profile details, quota history, and synced cloud reports are permanently destroyed.

Processing & Verification Timeline

  1. We will send a brief confirmation challenge to your registered email to verify ownership.
  2. Upon confirmation, your Supabase auth account, profile, quotas, and synced reports will be permanently deleted via ON DELETE CASCADE.
  3. Requests are fulfilled within 7 business days.
  4. You will receive a final confirmation email once data is permanently purged.

Local Device Data Notice

Reports and message drafts created in Guest Mode reside entirely inside your device's sandboxed local SQLite database. They are never uploaded to any server. To delete local device data, either tap Clear Local Data in the app's Privacy Center or uninstall the application from Android System Settings.
